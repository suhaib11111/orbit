    Win every lecture

    1BeforeSkim slides, look up new words, write 2–3 questions.
    2DuringSit in front, key ideas only, mark “?”, ask one question.
    3AfterShort notes the same day, solve every “?”, tick your week.

    Study math so it sticks

    1. Understand first. One short video before exercises.
    2. Solve on paper. Every step by hand.
    3. Mastery rule. Move on at 4 out of 5 right alone.
    4. Mistake notebook. Reread it weekly.
    5. Friday self-test. 10 mixed problems, no notes.
